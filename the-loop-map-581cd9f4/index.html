The Loop Map

Map one loop. Pen and paper, first answers, no polish. Work backwards from the result, because the result is the part you can already see.

The loop turns one way. Belief becomes thought, thought becomes emotion, emotion becomes action, action becomes result, and the result becomes the life that proves the belief. You will travel it the other way, starting at the only station you can already see.
