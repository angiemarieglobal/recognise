The Belief List

Station five, and how to stop stalling there.

Before You Read It

Station five is where almost everyone stops.

Not because the belief is hard to find. Because it is embarrassingly plain when it arrives. It is usually six or seven words long, it sounds like something a tired child would say, and people look at it and think, that cannot be it, that is far too simple to have cost me this much.

It is it. They are always that plain. A belief survives thirty years unexamined precisely because it is too obvious to question.

So this is not a list to choose from. It is a list to be caught by.

Read it slowly. You are not looking for the one that sounds cleverest or the one you would be least ashamed of. You are looking for the one that makes your chest go tight, or the one you skimmed past a little too fast.

Then write it in your own words on station five. The wording matters more than the accuracy. Yours will have a particular phrase in it that mine does not.

      If None Of Them Fit

      Then yours is close to one of these and wearing different clothes, and that is worth more than a match.

      Go back to the one that was nearly right and ask what is wrong with it. The correction is usually the belief.

      And if you find yourself deciding which one you ought to have, stop. The one you ought to have is a thought. The one that made you go quiet is the belief.

      It will be too simple. That is how you know you have found it.
